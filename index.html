Document 01 / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Applies to MacroLens Web Application and Sync API

1. Data Stored in Your Browser (localStorage)

MacroLens caches your account session metadata, profile metrics (age, biological sex, height, weight, activity level, goal), daily meal entries, hydration logs, body weight entries, achievement states, and AI Coach thread history inside your browser's localStorage under caltrack_* keys. This enables immediate reads and resilience during transient network interruptions.

2. Account Sync and MySQL Storage via Vercel

When you sign up or sign in, your credentials are verified by serverless API endpoints hosted on Vercel and passwords are hashed with bcryptjs. An expiring, signed JWT session cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax) identifies your user account on subsequent API requests.

Your profile, per-item diary entries, water intake records, body weight history, stats, and AI Coach conversation threads synchronize over HTTPS via /api/data/sync to a managed MySQL database so you can access your records across devices using timestamped per-record merging.

3. Meal Photos, Text Queries, and AI Coach (Gemini API)

When you run a natural language food parse, upload a meal photo, or send a message in the AI Coach view, the request goes from your browser to MacroLens's authenticated /api/gemini endpoint, which forwards the prompt (and compressed image data or summary of your logged nutrition metrics for coaching) to Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash API.

Your meal description or image payload is transmitted strictly to generate nutritional estimates or coaching replies. Unauthenticated requests to /api/gemini are rejected, and compressed meal thumbnails stored in your diary remain inside your personal diary record only.

4. Packaged Food Barcode Lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you scan a barcode with your camera or type a barcode number, your browser queries the public Open Food Facts API (world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product) using only the barcode string to retrieve product name, serving weight, calories, macros, micronutrients, and public product image URLs.

5. Analytics, Cookies, and Data Deletion

MacroLens uses no third-party advertising cookies or behavioral tracking pixels. CSV and PDF report exports are generated locally inside your browser without uploading files to external servers.

You can permanently delete all synced account data (diary items, hydration records, weight logs, coach threads, and preferences) at any time using the Reset Data action in the app header, or sign out to clear the active session cookie.

Terms and Conditions

Applies to usage of MacroLens Calorie & Macro Tracker

1. Informational Estimates, Not Medical Advice

MacroLens provides daily energy expenditure formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor TDEE), macronutrient targets, hydration goals, micronutrient thresholds, and AI-assisted food estimates solely for personal fitness tracking and informational logging.

Outputs from the app, including AI Coach replies, do not constitute medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or dietary prescription. Always consult a qualified physician or registered dietitian regarding medical conditions, allergies, eating disorders, or specialized nutrition needs.

2. Accuracy of AI Parsing and Third-Party Labels

  • AI Photo & Text Estimates: Dish recognition and gram estimates powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash are approximations and depend on image clarity, hidden fats or sauces, and portion descriptions. You are encouraged to review and adjust ingredient weights before logging.
  • Open Food Facts Barcode Data: Packaged product facts rely on publicly contributed Open Food Facts data and manufacturer packaging updates, which may vary by region or reformulation.

3. Account Responsibility and Acceptable Use

You are responsible for choosing a strong password and maintaining control of your signed-in browser sessions. Automated scraping, credential stuffing, bypassing authentication controls, or sending excessive automated payloads to the /api/gemini or sync endpoints is prohibited and subject to rate limiting.

MacroLens is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis without warranties of uninterrupted uptime or exact caloric precision. Features may be updated, rate-limited, or modified over time.
